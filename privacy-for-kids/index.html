ChorePup Privacy — A Short Guide for Kids

A shorter, friendlier explanation of what ChorePup saves and how kids can ask a grown-up for help.

ChorePup helps your family keep track of chores, points, routines, and rewards.

A grown-up is in charge

Your parent or guardian sets up ChorePup. They choose your profile and decide which family members and devices can use it.

What ChorePup saves

ChorePup may save:

  • The nickname and emoji your grown-up chose for you;
  • Your chores, routines, points, goals, and rewards;
  • Requests you send to your grown-up;
  • Notes you choose to add for your family; and
  • Information needed to keep a linked device working safely.

You do not have to write a note to finish a chore or ask for a reward.

Who can see it

Approved people in your private ChorePup family can see family information based on their role. ChorePup does not give you a public profile or let strangers chat with you.

What ChorePup does not do

ChorePup does not show ads, sell your information, or ask for your school, exact location, medical information, money information, fingerprints, face scan, government ID, or a photo of you.

Never put private information in a chore, reward, routine, or note.

You can ask for help

Ask your grown-up if you want to:

  • See what ChorePup knows about your profile;
  • Fix something that is wrong;
  • Delete a note or other information;
  • Stop using a linked device; or
  • Ask to delete your ChorePup profile.

Your grown-up can use the privacy and deletion tools in ChorePup or email support@xenithhorizon.com.

The complete grown-up Privacy Policy is available inside ChorePup and at: https://www.xenithhorizon.com/privacy
