<section class="chorepup-section">
  <h2>Verification</h2>

  <div class="chorepup-card">
    <p>Because ChorePup is a family app, some data may belong to or affect more than one family member. We may need to verify that you are authorized to delete the requested account, family, or family data before completing the request.</p>
    <p>Verification may include confirming the email address associated with your account, confirming family details, or requesting additional information needed to confirm that the request is legitimate.</p>
    <p><strong>Do not send passwords or PINs.</strong></p>
  </div>
</section>
