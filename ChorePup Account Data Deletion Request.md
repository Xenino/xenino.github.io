# ChorePup Account and Data Deletion Request

ChorePup is operated by Xenith Horizon Group LLC.

Parents may request deletion of their ChorePup account data and family data by emailing:

**support@xenithhorizon.com**

## What to include

Please include the following information in your request when available:

- The email address linked to your ChorePup parent account.
- Your family ID, if available.
- Whether you want to delete only your parent account access or the full family data connected to the account.

## Verification

For security, we may need to verify that you are authorized to request deletion of the account or family data before completing the request.

## What may be deleted

Depending on your request and your account permissions, deletion may include:

- Parent account connection data.
- Family setup information.
- Child profiles created by the parent.
- Chores, routines, goals, rewards, point requests, and transaction history.
- Other app data connected to the family account.

## What may be retained

Some limited information may be retained if needed for legal, security, fraud-prevention, tax, accounting, dispute-resolution, or compliance purposes.

## Processing time

We will respond to deletion requests as soon as reasonably possible after receiving and verifying the request.
