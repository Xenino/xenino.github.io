# ChorePup Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** June 24, 2026

ChorePup is designed for families to manage chores, routines, points, rewards, approvals, and allowance or screen-time motivation. We aim to collect only the information needed to make the app work for families.

## 1. Who We Are

ChorePup is provided by Xenith Horizon.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request account or data deletion, contact us at:

[support@xenithhorizon.com](mailto:support@xenithhorizon.com)

## 2. Information We Collect

ChorePup collects information needed to create and manage family chore and reward experiences. The exact information collected may depend on which features a family chooses to use.

### Parent or Adult Account Information

When a parent or adult family member creates or joins a family, we may collect:

* Email address, if provided through Google Sign-In or account linking
* Display name, if provided through Google Sign-In or entered in the app
* Firebase Authentication user ID or similar account identifier
* Family membership role, such as Owner, Co-owner, or Parent

### Family Setup Information

To support family setup and family syncing, we may collect:

* Family name
* Family ID
* Family PIN information stored in protected or hashed form
* Family member roles and permissions
* Family settings

### Child Profile Information

ChorePup is designed to use minimal child profile information. Parents may create child profiles using:

* Parent-assigned child display names or nicknames
* Emoji or app-provided profile visuals
* Point balances, goals, reward preferences, tasks, routines, and approval history

ChorePup does not require a child’s full legal name, precise location, school information, medical information, or photos.

### Chore, Routine, Reward, and Approval Data

To operate the app, we may collect and store:

* Tasks, chores, and routine names
* Reward names and reward point costs
* Point transactions and point balances
* Assigned tasks and routine completion status
* Approval requests, retry requests, approvals, denials, and related notes
* Reward goals and progress
* Dates and timestamps associated with app activity

### Device and Technical Information

ChorePup may use Firebase and Google services that process technical information needed to provide authentication, syncing, security, and app functionality. This may include identifiers such as Firebase installation or authentication identifiers, device type, app version, and basic diagnostic information generated by those services.

## 3. Photos, Videos, Audio, Contacts, and Location

ChorePup does not require families to upload child photos, videos, or audio.

ChorePup does not collect address book contacts.

ChorePup does not collect or share precise physical location.

Families should not enter precise location details, school information, medical information, financial account information, passwords, or other sensitive details into names, notes, tasks, rewards, routines, or other free-text fields.

## 4. Family-Created Content and Private Family Sharing

ChorePup allows parents and family members to create private family content inside the app. This may include child profile display names or nicknames, emojis, task names, routines, rewards, goals, approval requests, parent responses, notes, and point transaction history.

This content is intended only for use within the private family group connected to the ChorePup family account. ChorePup does not provide public profiles, public posting, public feeds, public chat, public image sharing, or communication with strangers.

Parents control family setup, family access, child profiles, approvals, rewards, routines, and administrative roles. Parents are responsible for the information they enter or allow within their family account.

Families should not enter sensitive personal information, inappropriate content, bullying or harassing language, precise location details, school information, medical information, financial account information, or other unnecessary personal details into names, notes, tasks, rewards, routines, or other free-text fields.

If we become aware of content or account activity that may violate our policies, harm users, threaten security, or violate the law, we may restrict access, remove content, or take other appropriate action.

## 5. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

* Create and manage family accounts
* Allow parents to create child profiles, tasks, chores, routines, and rewards
* Track points, reward goals, approvals, and transaction history
* Sync family data across devices
* Support parent and child app experiences
* Maintain account security and family access controls
* Respond to support, privacy, and deletion requests
* Improve reliability and troubleshoot app issues

## 6. How We Share Information

ChorePup does not sell user data.

ChorePup does not use advertising SDKs and does not share personal information for third-party advertising.

Family content may be visible to other members of the same private family group, depending on their role and app permissions. ChorePup does not make family content publicly searchable or publicly visible.

We may share or process information through service providers needed to operate the app, such as Firebase and Google services used for authentication, cloud storage, syncing, and app distribution. These providers process information according to their own privacy and security terms.

We may disclose information if required by law, to protect users, to investigate misuse, to respond to valid legal requests, or to protect the security and integrity of ChorePup.

## 7. Children and Families

ChorePup is a family app intended to be managed by parents or guardians. Parents create and manage child profiles and control how the app is used within their family.

Child profiles are designed to use minimal information. Parents should use nicknames or display names instead of full legal names when possible and should avoid entering sensitive child information.

ChorePup does not provide public communication features for children and does not allow children to communicate with strangers through the app.

## 8. Data Security

We use Firebase and Google services to support authentication, cloud syncing, and data storage. Data transmitted between the app and cloud services is encrypted in transit.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We work to use reasonable safeguards appropriate for a family chore and rewards app, but we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Data Retention

We keep app data for as long as needed to provide ChorePup functionality, support family syncing, maintain account security, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our policies.

Some app data, such as transaction history, approval history, family membership records, or support request records, may be retained for a period of time after account or data deletion if needed for security, legal compliance, troubleshooting, backup recovery, or protection of users.

## 10. Account and Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their ChorePup account and associated app data by using the account deletion request page linked in the app and on the Google Play listing.

Users may also request deletion of some or all app data without deleting their account. For example, a user may request removal of a family, child profile, task history, reward history, approval notes, or other app data where deletion is technically and legally possible.

Because ChorePup is designed for family use, some records may be connected to multiple family members. We may need to verify that the requester is authorized to delete the requested account, family, or family data before completing the request.

Deleted data may include account records, family membership records, child profiles, tasks, routines, rewards, goals, approval requests, notes, and point transaction history associated with the verified request.

Some data may be retained when necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, troubleshooting, backup recovery, or to protect the rights and safety of users. Backup copies may remain for a limited period before being deleted through normal backup rotation.

Deletion requests are typically reviewed and processed within 30 days after we receive enough information to verify and complete the request.

To request account or data deletion, visit:

[ChorePup Account and Data Deletion Request](ChorePup%20Account%20Data%20Deletion%20Request.md)

## 11. Your Choices

Parents can manage family information inside the app, including child profiles, tasks, routines, rewards, approvals, and certain family settings.

You may contact us to request access, correction, deletion, or help with your ChorePup data:

[support@xenithhorizon.com](mailto:support@xenithhorizon.com)

## 12. Third-Party Services

ChorePup may use third-party services necessary to operate the app, including Firebase, Google Sign-In, Google Play, and related Google services. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

ChorePup does not use advertising SDKs, behavioral advertising, or third-party tracking for advertising purposes.

## 13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of ChorePup after an update means the updated policy applies.

## 14. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, ChorePup privacy practices, or account and data deletion, contact us at:

[support@xenithhorizon.com](mailto:support@xenithhorizon.com)
