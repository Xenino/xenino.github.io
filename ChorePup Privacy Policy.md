<section class="chorepup-section">
  <h2>12. Account and Data Deletion</h2>

  <div class="chorepup-card">
    <p>Users may request deletion of their ChorePup account and associated app data by using the account deletion request page linked in the app and on the Google Play listing.</p>
    <p>Users may also request deletion of some or all app data without deleting their account. For example, a user may request removal of a family, child profile, task history, reward history, approval notes, or other app data where deletion is technically and legally possible.</p>
    <p>Because ChorePup is designed for family use, some records may be connected to multiple family members. We may need to verify that the requester is authorized to delete the requested account, family, or family data before completing the request.</p>
    <p>Deleted data may include account records, family membership records, child profiles, tasks, routines, rewards, goals, approval requests, notes, and point transaction history associated with the verified request.</p>
    <p>Some data may be retained when necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, troubleshooting, backup recovery, or to protect the rights and safety of users. Backup copies may remain for a limited period before being deleted through normal backup rotation.</p>
    <p>Deletion requests are typically reviewed and processed within 30 days after we receive enough information to verify and complete the request.</p>
