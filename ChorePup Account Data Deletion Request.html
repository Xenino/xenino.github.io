ChorePup allows users to request deletion of their account and associated app data. Users may also request deletion of some or all app data without deleting their account.

How to Request Deletion

To request account or data deletion, email ChorePup support using the button below. The email template will help you include the details needed to process your request.

Request Account or Data Deletion

If the email button does not open correctly, send your request manually to:

support@xenithhorizon.com

What to Include

Please include the following information in your request:

  • Request type: account deletion or data deletion only
  • Email address used for ChorePup or Google Sign-In
  • Family ID, if known
  • Family name, if known
  • A short description of what you want deleted

Please do not include sensitive child information, school information, medical information, financial account information, passwords, PINs, or precise location details.

Copy/Paste Request Template

You can copy and paste this template into your email if needed:

Request type: Delete my account and associated data / Delete some or all app data only ``` Email used for ChorePup or Google Sign-In: Family ID, if known: Family name, if known: What data should be deleted: I understand that ChorePup may need to verify that I am authorized to request deletion of the account, family, or family data listed above.
```

Types of Requests

You may request:

  • Deletion of your ChorePup account and associated app data
  • Deletion of some or all ChorePup app data without deleting your account
  • Deletion of a family, child profile, task history, reward history, approval notes, or other app data where deletion is technically and legally possible

What Data May Be Deleted

Depending on your request and verification, deleted data may include:

  • Account records
  • Family membership records
  • Child profiles
  • Tasks and chores
  • Routines
  • Rewards and goals
  • Approval requests and notes
  • Point transaction history
  • Family settings associated with the verified request

Verification

Because ChorePup is a family app, some data may belong to or affect more than one family member. We may need to verify that you are authorized to delete the requested account, family, or family data before completing the request.

Verification may include confirming the email address associated with your account, confirming family details, or requesting additional information needed to confirm that the request is legitimate. Do not send passwords or PINs.

Data That May Be Retained

Some data may be retained when necessary for:

  • Security
  • Fraud prevention
  • Legal compliance
  • Troubleshooting
  • Backup recovery
  • Protecting the rights and safety of users

Backup copies may remain for a limited period before being deleted through normal backup rotation.

Processing Time

Deletion requests are typically reviewed and processed within 30 days after we receive enough information to verify and complete the request.

Questions

For privacy or deletion questions, contact:

support@xenithhorizon.com

ChorePup does not sell user data and does not use advertising SDKs or behavioral advertising.
